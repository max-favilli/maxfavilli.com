His case rests on Anthropic's prospectus. There isn't a public one. The draft was filed confidentially in June and nothing has appeared on EDGAR since. He is quoting a leaked copy — and so am I.

He is right about two things. A quarter of 2025 revenue came from two customers, and both are owned by competitors' parents. The compute commitments don't flex if growth does.

He is wrong that the customers aren't there. Revenue went from $4.6 billion across 2025 to $11.5 billion in the second quarter of 2026 alone, and a $65 billion annualised run rate by July.

And this is not SpaceX 2.0. That prospectus had asteroid mining in it. This one has customers.

The risk I take seriously is China — but not because your IT department will start self-hosting. That is worse value than buying the tokens. It is because a cheap provider can undercut everyone on hosted inference.
