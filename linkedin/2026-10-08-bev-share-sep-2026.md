## Post body

Is this the acceleration point?

Nine months of national registration data, nine markets.

→ Finland: 62% of new cars in September were battery-electric. More than half for the whole quarter.
→ Germany: up almost every month this year. 22.0% in January, 34.5% in September.
→ France: +10 points from Q2 to Q3. Germany +5.5, Finland +6.7, Spain +3.7. The biggest step of the year.
→ Sweden at 47%. Norway at 98.8%.
→ Italy is the exception: 7.6%, below its own average for the year.

New registrations, not cars on the road. The fleet changes far more slowly than the showroom.

But the showroom is moving faster now, not slower.
