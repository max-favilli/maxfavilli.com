𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗺𝗼𝘀𝘁 𝗿𝗶𝗴𝗼𝗿𝗼𝘂𝘀 𝘀𝘁𝘂𝗱𝘆 𝗼𝗻 𝗔𝗜 𝗰𝗼𝗱𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗳𝗼𝘂𝗻𝗱 𝗱𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗹𝗼𝗽𝗲𝗿𝘀 𝘄𝗲𝗿𝗲 𝗮𝗯𝗼𝘂𝘁 𝟮𝟬% 𝘀𝗹𝗼𝘄𝗲𝗿 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝗶𝘁.

I ship in half a day what used to take weeks.

Both are true.

The studies count merged pull requests and time on task. Those measure motion, not outcomes. If an agent lets one developer deliver a working product in two days instead of six weeks, PR throughput might not move at all — the unit of delivery changed, not the count.

Meanwhile the industry that sells software effort is quietly abandoning effort as a unit of billing.

When the people who sell hours stop pricing by the hour, believe them.
