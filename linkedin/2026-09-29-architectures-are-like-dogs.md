𝗔𝗿𝗰𝗵𝗶𝘁𝗲𝗰𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗲𝘀 𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝗹𝗶𝗸𝗲 𝗱𝗼𝗴𝘀 — 𝘁𝗵𝗲𝘆 𝗿𝗲𝘀𝗲𝗺𝗯𝗹𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗶𝗿 𝗼𝘄𝗻𝗲𝗿𝘀.

Melvin Conway, 1968: any organisation that designs a system will inevitably produce a design whose structure is a copy of the organisation’s communication structure.

Look at any system old enough to have survived a reorg. The architecture is a fossil record of who used to talk to whom.

If structure follows communication whether you intend it or not, then the org chart is an architectural document.

Jonny LeRoy and Matt Simons named this the inverse Conway maneuver in 2010. Stop drawing the architecture you want. Start drawing the teams that would produce it.

Choose the team. The architecture follows.
