Every figure here is the battery-electric share of new cars registered that month, taken from each country's own statistics.

- **The rise is broad, and it is speeding up.** Germany, France, Finland and Sweden rose in every quarter of 2026. Norway and Denmark wobbled, but at 80–99% there is little room left to rise. The step from Q2 to Q3 was the largest so far: France +10 points, Finland +6.7, Germany +5.5, Spain +3.7.
- **Germany is not one good September.** Its share rose almost every month: 22.0% in January, 21.9, 24.0, 25.8, 25.0, 28.4, 29.3, 32.4, and 34.5% in September.
- **Finland crossed a real threshold.** More than half of new cars in Q3 were battery-electric, and 62% in September. Sweden is at 47%, on the official statistics.
- **Italy is the exception.** 7.6% in September, below its own average for the year.

These are new registrations, not cars on the road. A car stays in service for well over a decade, so the fleet changes far more slowly than the showroom.

*Sources: KBA, Traficom / Statistics Finland, SSB, Statistics Denmark, SCB, PFA / CCFA, ANFAC, UNRAE, CPCA. Figures retrieved 8 October 2026; current-year data are provisional.*
